# Mix Cover Concepts

All covers are square music thumbnails for the 5th Dimension archive. The shared visual system is **print-worn pirate-broadcast collage**: graphite base, parchment paper, Signal Orange `#ff5a1f` as the functional cue, limited acid green metadata marks, tactile halftone grain, misregistered ink, technical-rule fragments, and a small abstract 5D seal. To preserve flexibility and avoid unreliable generated lettering, images contain **no title or artist text**; the website and approval presentation provide that metadata.
