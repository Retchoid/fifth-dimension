# Visual Reference Research — August 2026

## Sources reviewed

| Source | Relevant evidence | Design implication for 5th Dimension |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AIGA Eye on Design: *Vaporwave is Dead, Long Live Vaporwave*](https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/vaporwave-is-dead-long-live-vaporwave/) | Describes vaporwave visual language as neon-lit, digitally constructed nostalgia using retro games, television, old-school word art, saccharine colour, and an intentionally unsettling consumer-tech gloss. | Keep the existing cyan/magenta signal palette, but deploy it as sparse, high-energy signal light over graphite hardware and distorted archival textures—not as a default border colour on every surface. |
| [Northwestern University: British Rave Flyers Collection](https://findingaids.library.northwestern.edu/repositories/7/resources/1994) | The archive covers 746 illustrated flyers and postcards from 1990s rave/nightclub culture; the material combines vivid graphics with venue, theme, promoter, price, and DJ-lineup information. | Treat the page as a navigable transmission flyer: preserve the existing factual archive information, but establish hierarchy with stamped metadata, variable display type, ticket-like identifiers, and disciplined information density. |
| [Dazed: *Classic Jungle Flyers from the Rave Archive*](https://www.dazeddigital.com/artsandculture/article/19746/1/dizzys-jungle-flyers) | Documents original jungle flyers and describes jungle as a fast, bass-heavy blend of hip-hop/reggae sampling and drums with MC culture at its centre. | Use graffiti and sound-system cues as cultural framing rather than decoration: strong black contour, layered pasted-paper depth, stencilled utility copy, speaker-led focal points, and selective high-chroma impact fields. |

## Proposed synthesis

The redesign should avoid flattening the site into generic vaporwave. It should combine **rave-flyer information density**, **jungle sound-system materiality**, **urban decay texture**, and **controlled cyber-vapor glow**. Album art remains untouched and functions as the authentic colour source in the archive. The 5D Playa should read as a removable battered radio/console module rather than a glossy software widget.

The planned audit will specifically check that the existing media, descriptions, adaptive-card metadata, individual player controls, downloads, shares, release gate, arcade controls, and database-backed leaderboard stay unchanged before any approved visual implementation begins.
